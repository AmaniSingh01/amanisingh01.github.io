01 · Project 01

From fragmented sheets to a central workflow

The documented design addresses overlapping spreadsheets, duplicated logic, job-wise tabs and sensitive information mixed into operational tracking.

Problem context

Operational complexity

  • Recruitment, onboarding, contracts, compliance, reminders and finance were spread across overlapping workbooks.
  • Job-wise tabs and duplicated formulas made the system difficult to maintain and scale.
  • Sensitive identity, tax and bank information sat alongside everyday operational data.
  • Different sheets could become competing sources of operational truth.
Design principle

Separate inputs, decisions and outputs

Raw data is captured once, compliance information is consolidated, and one central tracker determines the candidate’s operational state. Downstream sheets consume the resulting outputs rather than recreating the logic.

Core rule: MASTER_TRACKER_V2 is the central decision layer for operational stage, compliance state, payment eligibility, reminders and hold reasons.

02 · System components

How the workflow is organised

The architecture separates the responsibilities of each workbook and sheet to reduce duplicated business logic and control access to sensitive records.

Operations hub

Candidate operations & central tracker

  • ONBOARDED_CANDIDATES captures candidate, client/project, status, rates and contract metadata.
  • MASTER_TRACKER_V2 consolidates identity and operational state.
  • Event triggers move candidates into pre-trial, contract or payment stages.
  • REMINDER_QUEUE extracts records requiring follow-up.
Compliance vault

Separated sensitive documentation

  • Domestic and international form responses are brought into CANDIDATE_RESPONSES_MASTER.
  • Personal email is used as the matching key in the documented design.
  • Contract requirements differ for domestic and international candidates.
  • Payment compliance is checked only after a timesheet is received.
Finance layer

Timesheet & payment processing

  • TIMESHEET consumes approved hours, candidate rate, currency and payment eligibility.
  • Total payable is calculated as approved hours × candidate rate.
  • PAYMENT_TRACKER records payable amount, expected payment date and final payment status.
  • Ineligible payments are marked On Hold; otherwise finance status is carried forward.
Communication layer

Event-driven reminders & mail preparation

  • Pre-trial reminders cover Slack, Jibble, onboarding meetings and software setup.
  • Contract-stage reminders cover signing and onboarding documents.
  • Payment-stage reminders cover TIN, TRC, Form 41, No PE and payment processing.
  • Mailmeteor queue formats approved outputs for communication.

02 · Project 02

Recruitment operations, performance visibility & specialist sourcing

A combined engagement focused on improving day-to-day recruitment execution, making pipeline performance easier to monitor and reaching candidates for specialist roles.

Operating challenge

Execution without consolidated visibility

  • Recruitment processes had been running in established ways and needed a fresh operational review.
  • Performance information existed, but leadership did not have a consolidated view of key funnel metrics.
  • Task prioritisation and delegation needed more structure as recruitment activity scaled.
  • Candidate communication required better stage awareness and consistent follow-up.
My contribution

Process improvement and hands-on delivery

  • Reviewed recruitment operations and supported workflow streamlining, task prioritisation and delegation.
  • Conducted structured candidate outreach using LinkedIn Recruiter, CEIPAL ATS and Gmail.
  • Personally conducted approximately 10 candidate interviews through Google Meet.
  • Supported a new initiative and built a project website using Hostinger’s AI-assisted website builder.
Performance visibility

Recruitment KPI dashboards

Built Google Sheets dashboards to consolidate six recruitment funnel indicators into a view that could be read at a glance.

Recruitment operating model

Before → after

  • Visibility: metrics compiled manually on request → six-KPI dashboard.
  • Prioritisation: ad hoc task handling → structured prioritisation and delegation.
  • Outreach: uniform messaging → status-aware, template-driven communication.
  • Web presence: no dedicated workstream website → website built and launched.

03 · Portfolio reflection

What this work demonstrates

Process thinking

Diagnosing operational bottlenecks, mapping candidate journeys and translating recurring work into defined stages, triggers and outputs.

Systems design

Separating source data, decision logic, operational views, finance processing and communication while building repeatable recruitment workflows.

Operational control

Improving visibility of candidate status, missing requirements, reminders, recruitment funnel performance and payment holds.
