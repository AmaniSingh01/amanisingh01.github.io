Seasonality-led activation

Peak demand: wedding seasons around April–June and October–January.

Quiet period: July–August is used for supplier acquisition and building creative assets.

Timing: launch in Delhi-NCR in March 2027, with Bengaluru expansion planned for September 2027.

The plan moves some spend ahead of Oct–Dec to avoid higher auction costs and capture pre-event planning behaviour.

Strategic conclusions

WHAT THE RESEARCH SUPPORTS

Recommended priorities

01. Use creator-led storytelling to make rental an attractive option, not just a cheaper alternative.

02. Build occasion-specific landing pages that answer fit, hygiene, pricing and deposit concerns.

03. Convert interest through personal stylist consultations rather than a conventional checkout-only flow.

04. Tie spend to completed rentals, then use repeat and referral loops to reduce acquisition cost.

Assumptions and limitations

The project is a proposed strategy, not evidence of campaign performance. Rental targets, funnel conversion rates, channel economics and market share are planning assumptions. The competitor map is qualitative. The positioning and funnel need validation through renter interviews and real campaign data; some wedding-window dates also require verification before activation.

The managerial question is not simply “which channel gets reach?” It is which combination of content, trust and conversion design produces completed rentals at an improving cost.
